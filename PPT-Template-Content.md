## Slide 5: Problem Statements for Participants

### **Track 1: Education & Learning**
**Problem 1: Smart Learning Management System**
- Create an AI-powered platform that personalizes learning paths for students
- Features: Adaptive content, progress tracking, interactive assessments
- Target: Students, teachers, educational institutions

**Problem 2: Virtual Lab Assistant**
- Develop a VR/AR solution for conducting science experiments remotely
- Features: 3D simulations, real-time data collection, safety protocols
- Target: Science students, researchers, educators

### **Track 2: Healthcare & Wellness**
**Problem 3: Mental Health Companion App**
- Build an app that provides mental health support and resources
- Features: Mood tracking, meditation guides, crisis intervention
- Target: General public, mental health professionals

**Problem 4: Telemedicine Platform**
- Create a comprehensive telemedicine solution for remote consultations
- Features: Video calls, prescription management, health records
- Target: Patients, doctors, healthcare providers

### **Track 3: Environment & Sustainability**
**Problem 5: Carbon Footprint Tracker**
- Develop a system to track and reduce individual/organizational carbon footprint
- Features: Data visualization, reduction suggestions, goal setting
- Target: Individuals, companies, environmental organizations

**Problem 6: Smart Waste Management**
- Create an IoT-based waste management system for cities
- Features: Smart bins, route optimization, recycling tracking
- Target: Municipal corporations, waste management companies

### **Track 4: Social Impact & Community**
**Problem 7: Community Safety Network**
- Build a platform for neighborhood safety and emergency response
- Features: Incident reporting, emergency alerts, community coordination
- Target: Residents, local authorities, emergency services

**Problem 8: Skill Sharing Platform**
- Develop a peer-to-peer skill learning and teaching platform
- Features: Skill matching, video tutorials, progress tracking
- Target: Students, professionals, hobbyists
